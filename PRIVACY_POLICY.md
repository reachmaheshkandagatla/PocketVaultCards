# Privacy Policy for PocketVault Cards

**Effective date:** 24 July 2026
**Last updated:** 24 July 2026

PocketVault Cards ("the App") is an offline personal-vault application provided by OngeLabs ("OngeLabs", "we", "us", or "our"), based in South Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Privacy Summary

PocketVault Cards is designed to keep your information on your device. OngeLabs does not operate an App server and does not collect, receive, sell, rent, or use the content you save in the App. The App does not request general internet access.

Information leaves the App only when you deliberately use a feature that relies on an Android or Google Play service, such as document scanning, checking for an update, or sharing an item with an application you select.

## 2. Information You May Store

You may choose to save information such as:

- folder, card, and coupon names;
- card and coupon images;
- grocery items, quantities, completion states, and reminder settings;
- bank-card details and PINs; and
- scanned receipts, bills, or other documents.

This content is stored locally in the App’s private storage on your device. OngeLabs does not receive or have remote access to it and cannot recover it for you.

## 3. Information Collected by OngeLabs

OngeLabs does not use the App to collect:

- your saved vault content;
- names, email addresses, or contact lists;
- precise or approximate location;
- advertising identifiers or data for advertising;
- analytics or App-usage profiles;
- device identifiers; or
- biometric information.

The App does not contain advertising and OngeLabs does not sell personal information or use saved content for profiling or marketing.

## 4. Device Permissions

### Camera

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a card, receipt, or bill. The App does not continuously access the camera. You may deny or revoke camera permission through Android settings, although scanning features may then be unavailable.

### Notifications

Notification permission is used only for grocery reminders that you enable. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and are not used for advertising or tracking. You can disable reminders in the App or revoke notification permission through Android settings.

## 5. Document Scanning and Google Play Services

The App uses the Google Play services document scanner to provide its scanning interface. When you request a scan, Google Play services processes the camera interaction and returns the resulting document to the App for local storage.

Google Play services and the Google Play Store may process limited device, service, security, or diagnostic information under Google’s own terms and privacy practices. OngeLabs does not receive the document or diagnostic information from Google through this feature. For more information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The App also uses Google Play’s in-app update service when you ask it to check for an available update. Google Play handles that request under Google’s privacy practices.

## 6. Biometric and Device-Credential Authentication

The App can use Android’s biometric or device-credential system to protect access to the vault and sensitive details. Authentication is performed by Android. The App receives only the result needed to allow or deny access.

OngeLabs does not receive or store your fingerprint, facial image, biometric template, screen-lock PIN, pattern, or password.

## 7. Sharing Information

Nothing is shared automatically. When you deliberately choose **Share**, the App gives the selected item to Android’s system share sheet. The item is then sent only to the application or service you select.

The selected recipient handles the shared copy under its own privacy policy and security practices. OngeLabs does not control independently selected receiving applications. To delete a shared copy, you must manage it in the receiving application or service.

## 8. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App without retaining its data.

You can:

- delete individual records using the App’s delete controls;
- clear all App data through Android’s application settings; or
- uninstall the App and choose to delete its data.

Cloud backup and Android device-to-device transfer are disabled for the App. On supported Android devices, the uninstall screen may nevertheless offer an option to retain App data. If you keep that data and later reinstall the same signed application, Android may make the retained information available again on that device.

Because OngeLabs does not possess a server-side copy of your vault, we cannot remotely view, recover, correct, export, or delete that locally stored content.

## 9. Security

The App uses safeguards that include:

- SQLCipher encryption for the Room database;
- AES-256-GCM encryption for saved card and bill images;
- a non-exportable Android Keystore key that protects encryption key material;
- Android private application storage;
- optional biometric or device-credential access control;
- screenshot and screen-recording protection on sensitive detail screens;
- disabled cloud backup and device-to-device transfer;
- no general internet permission; and
- Android’s controlled file-sharing mechanism for user-initiated sharing.

No storage or security system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device credentials, keeping your device and operating system updated, and carefully selecting any application with which you share information.

## 10. Legal Disclosure

OngeLabs does not ordinarily possess your saved vault information and therefore generally has no server-side copy to disclose. We may disclose information that we do possess, such as correspondence you voluntarily send to our support email, if reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a lawful government request, or to protect legal rights and safety.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

PocketVault Cards is a general productivity utility and is not directed to children. OngeLabs does not knowingly use the App to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to OngeLabs, contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information held by OngeLabs. OngeLabs operates from South Africa and handles personal information it actually receives in accordance with applicable South African law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Most information used in PocketVault Cards remains solely on your device and is controlled directly through the App and Android settings. For information you send directly to OngeLabs, you may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable law.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will show a new "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects how the App handles information, we will provide additional notice where appropriate.

We will update this policy before introducing features such as accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, or other remote data processing.
